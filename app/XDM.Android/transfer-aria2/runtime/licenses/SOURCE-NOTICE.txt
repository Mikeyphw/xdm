Release/distribution bytes are built by XDM from pinned source archives rather than copied
from a third-party Android binary release. The authoritative source URLs and SHA-256 values
are stored in transfer-aria2/runtime/aria2-runtime.json and repeated in the generated
aria2-runtime.lock.json together with measured NDK/toolchain fingerprints and final ELF
security metadata.
